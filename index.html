I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Virginia, where I have the privilege of being advised by Prof. Aidong Zhang (Fellow of ACM/IEEE/AIMBE) and Prof. Henry Kautz (Fellow of ACM/AAAS/AAAI). I am actively seeking full-time roles in academia (tenure-track Assistant Professor) and industry (AI Research Scientist). Please feel free to contact me.

Research Interests

My north star is trustworthy AI that can safely learn recursively and drive new scientific discovery from AGI to ASI. Below are the subtopics I have been working on.

  • ML Foundation: OOD generalization, spurious correlations and Bayesian machine learning. Representative papers: Evidential Alignment, LRFormer, NeuronTune, ShortcutProbe.
  • Safety Alignment: emergent/persistent misalignment in LLM agents. Representative papers: PRISM, AEA, SAGE, MM-SpuBench.
  • AI for Scientific Discovery: recursively self-improving (RSI) agents, learning foundation in RLVR, and scientific discovery. Representative papers: ProofEvolve, DFE, EHRMamba, MpoxVLM.
  • Applications: embodied AI and autonomous driving, and AI for health. Representative papers: LaMPilot, MAPLM, MaRs-VQA.

For a full list of papers, see the Publications page.
